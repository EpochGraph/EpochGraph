If you find an error

The facts in the graphs come from Wikidata, and the connections come from Wikipedia articles. If you find an error, correcting it on Wikidata or Wikipedia will bring the fix into EpochGraph at the next update. Updates are irregular. Each timeline page shows the date of its data (Wikidata and Wikipedia).

Requests to remove content

If you are an individual or a company and strongly wish to have content removed, please contact us at the email address above. We will take care of it. Including the following information helps us check your request quickly.

  • The URL of the page concerned (the timeline and period, or the name of the item)
  • The reason you would like it removed
  • If the request comes from the person concerned or someone connected to them, a note saying so

What happens after we receive your request

  1. We check the details.
  2. We remove the content concerned. When the matter is urgent, for example because it affects the privacy or reputation of a living person, we remove it first, before checking.
  3. We let you know the result at the email address you contacted us from.

We use your contact details only to reply to and handle your inquiry (see thePrivacy Policy).
