Privacy Policy

EpochGraph (the “Operator”) handles the personal information of users of the website EpochGraph (the “Site”) as follows.

1. Information we collect

  • The Site has no user registration or login.
  • The Site uses the analytics of the service that delivers it (Cloudflare Web Analytics) to count the number of visits, the pages viewed, the referring sites, and the visitors' environment (such as the type of browser or device, and the country). This analytics does not use cookies and does not track individual users.
  • The service that delivers the Site (Cloudflare) may keep access logs (such as IP addresses, pages viewed, and dates and times) for delivery and security.
  • If you send us an inquiry by email, we receive your email address and the content of your inquiry.

2. How we use it

Information you give us in an inquiry is used only to reply and to handle requests such as the removal of content. After a request has been handled, we keep only what is needed as a record.

The results of the analytics are used only to understand how the Site is used and to improve it. They are not used in a way that identifies individuals.

3. Disclosure to third parties

Except as required by law, we do not give the personal information you provide to third parties without your consent.

4. External services

The images on the Site are loaded from Wikimedia Commons. When an image is shown, your browser connects to Wikimedia's servers (and sends information such as your IP address). This is governed by the Wikimedia Foundation Privacy Policy.

For the analytics, your browser connects to Cloudflare's servers. This is governed by theCloudflare Privacy Policy.

5. Contact

Please send any questions about this policy to info@EpochGraph.com.

6. Changes

The Operator may change this policy as needed. A changed policy takes effect when it is posted on the Site.

7. Language

This policy is a translation of the Japanese original. If the English and Japanese versions differ, the Japanese version prevails.
