EpochGraph is a temporal 3D knowledge graph built from Wikidata and Wikipedia. You can see how people, organizations, events, and places are connected, year by year.

View the timelines

FEATURES

Features of EpochGraph

  • Facts from Wikidata

    People, organizations, events, places, and their years come from Wikidata, a public database that anyone can check.

  • Connections with sources

    Connections between people, and between people and events, are taken from the text of Wikipedia articles, with the evidence sentence and a link to the article.

  • A 3D graph that changes by year

    Move the year slider, and only the people who were active and the events that were happening in that year come into view.

  • Runs on InterSystems IRIS

    Tens of thousands of items and millions of connections are handled in an InterSystems IRIS database.

ABOUT

About EpochGraph

EpochGraph is a site that turns historical timelines into temporal 3D graphs built from publicly available data. Because the graphs are generated automatically, they may contain errors or omissions. To find out how to correct errors or request the removal of content, see “If you find something wrong” below.

Learn moreContact

CORRECTIONS

If you find something wrong

The facts in the graphs come from Wikidata, and the connections come from Wikipedia articles. If you find an error, correcting it on Wikidata or Wikipedia will bring the fix into EpochGraph at the next update. Updates are irregular. Each timeline page shows the date of its data (Wikidata and Wikipedia).

If you are an individual or a company and strongly wish to have content removed, please get in touch through the Contact page. We will take care of it.

THANKS

To the Wikidata community

EpochGraph is built on the efforts of everyone who has kept the data on Wikidata consistent over many years. This site would not have been possible without you. We are deeply grateful.

Use in videos and live streams

You are welcome to use this site in video production and live streaming, such as on TV or YouTube (including commercial and monetized videos). When you do, please include the URL of this site (https://epochgraph.com/en/).

We do not allow the graphs or pages of this site to be collected in bulk by automated means (scraping) for commercial use. The parts derived from Wikidata (CC0) and Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0) can be used freely within the terms of their licenses; please obtain them from the original sites.
