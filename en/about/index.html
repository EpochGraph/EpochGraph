About EpochGraph

EpochGraph is a site that turns historical timelines into temporal 3D graphs (knowledge graphs). You can see, year by year, when and how people, organizations, events, and places were connected.

How it is made

  • Facts: People, organizations, events, places, and their years come from the public database Wikidata.
  • Connections: Connections between people, and between people and events, are taken from the text of Wikipedia articles, with the evidence sentence and a link to the article (source).
  • Selection: The items shown in a graph are chosen automatically by the same rules for every timeline, based on factors such as how many connections they have. For each timeline, the operator may specify items that must be shown or must not be shown.
  • Supplements: The operator may research and add values or items that are not in Wikidata. These are labeled as an “EpochGraph note” or an “EpochGraph item.” Some of them do not have sources yet; we are adding sources over time. Years chosen by generative AI from the text of an article are marked “estimated.”
  • System: The data is handled in an InterSystems IRIS database.

Because the graphs are generated automatically, they may contain errors or omissions. If you correct an error on Wikidata or Wikipedia, the fix will appear here at the next update.Updates are irregular. Each timeline page shows the date of its data (Wikidata and Wikipedia). If you are an individual or a company and strongly wish to have content removed, please get in touch through the Contact page. We will take care of it.
